# Game Design Notes

## Pillars

1. **Readable tactics.** A square grid with height, where facing and elevation matter.
2. **Story between battles.** Short dialogue scenes before and after each fight.
3. **Magitech fantasy.** Swords and sorcery meet the machines a vanished civilization left behind.

## World: Veyra

Long ago an unnamed civilization built the **Lattice**, a network of engines powered by **ember cores**. When it collapsed, the ruins scattered across the continent. People who can attune to a core's hum, called **Resonants**, can draw magic from it.

The **Iron Synod**, a theocratic order, banned old-world engines a century ago. It hunts down surviving cores and claims to destroy them. Rumor says the Synod keeps them instead.

## Cast (prologue)

| Character | Class | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Rhen | Lattice Knight | Front-line fighter who wears a salvaged engine-gauntlet. Leader of the party. |
| Isolde | Resonant | Scholar who can hear the cores. Area magic and healing. |
| Tobin | Coilgunner | Scout with a magnetic rifle. Long range, high mobility. |

**Opposition:** Synod Wardens (shield infantry), Arbalists (crossbows) and Cantors (chanting battle-mages).

## Prologue: The Ashford Crossing

The party is smuggling a live ember core out of the canyons when a Synod patrol catches them at a river bridge. Winning reveals that the Cantor signaled for reinforcements, which sets up chapter 1.
